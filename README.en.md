## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/hvRJCLFzcasrR4ia7z/giphy.gif" width="28" alt="acenando"> About

I turn business problems into technology solutions — from requirements gathering and architecture design through development, deployment and long-term evolution.

I work on internal process automation, corporate applications and solutions for operational demands, as well as industrial automation architecture, process analysis and internal consulting to identify digitalization opportunities.

I use **AI as an accelerator** across the development cycle — technical research, prototyping, implementation and evaluation of architectural alternatives — while keeping the focus on architecture, integration, security, reliability and maintainability.

> **Technology-agnostic** approach: the stack is chosen to fit the problem's requirements, instead of forcing the problem into a single language or framework.
